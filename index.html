Limitations
  • Camera phones must stay on, plugged in, camera page open in the foreground, screen on.
  • Both devices need internet. Camera access requires HTTPS.
  • Public PeerJS signaling can be slow or down; some NATs fail without TURN.
  • Event clips upload to your Supabase (or save locally if not configured). No always-on cloud DVR.
  • iPhone: locking/backgrounding stops the camera stream. MediaRecorder on iOS Safari is limited (prefer Chrome/Android for camera phones).
  • Only monitor property you own/control; follow local notice/recording laws.
